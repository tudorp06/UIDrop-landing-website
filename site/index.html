How it works

Three steps from inspiration to build.

No DevTools. No manual inspection. Just click, read, and build.

01

Browse to any page

Find a site with a design language you want to learn from — a product, a portfolio, anything live in your browser.

02

Click Snap

UIDrop reads the rendered DOM in under a second — extracting colors, fonts, spacing, shadows, radii, gradients, and component specs.

03

Send to your AI

One click opens Claude or ChatGPT with the full design brief pre-loaded — or copy to clipboard for Cursor. Your AI starts building immediately.
